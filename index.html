About Us

At Mosana Cafe, we are passionate about serving the finest coffee and delectable pastries. Our mission is to create a welcoming space where you can relax, connect, and enjoy the best flavors in town. We are located in Kansanga along Ggaba road, next to the KIU girls hostel. Whether you're looking for a quick caffeine fix or a cozy spot to unwind, we've got you covered.
